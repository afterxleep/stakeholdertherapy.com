Everyone has values until they cost something.

They're easy to hold when they're free. When belief and convenience point the same way, you feel principled, but you're just comfortable.

But the bill always comes. Some day, doing the right thing will get expensive, and you'll discover what you actually believe versus what you were happy to say while it was cheap.

Most companies fold. Quietly reframe. The value gets "revisited," "matured," "aligned to reality." New bullshit language for the same retreat.

Conviction isn't what you write down when everything is good. It's what survives the first day it costs you money, time or real effort.

Hold something long enough to bleed for it. Then, only then, call it a fucking principle.
